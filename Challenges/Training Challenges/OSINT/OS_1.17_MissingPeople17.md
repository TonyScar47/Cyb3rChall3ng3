# OS_1.17 — Missing people 17

---

## Problem description

Seventeenth challenge in the "Missing people" series. It asks to determine the Facebook profile URL of Anthony's former girlfriend (`Kimberly`), identified in the previous challenge.

Flag format: `[https://www.facebook.com/USERNAME](https://www.facebook.com/USERNAME)`.

## Recon

The original target account on Facebook is no longer active or reachable online. Due to this link rot, the challenge has been permanently archived by the authors, and the valid resolution flag is directly embedded within the challenge description.

## Solution, step by step

1. Open the challenge details on the platform.
2. Read the prompt note stating that the target account is no longer available and that the challenge is archived.
3. Copy the resolution URL provided directly in the text: **`[https://www.facebook.com/kokim24](https://www.facebook.com/kokim24)`**.
